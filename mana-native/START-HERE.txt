MANA TABLE NATIVE - BUILD, TEST AND PLAY COMMANDER

This guide follows the source revision recorded by the package. Check SOURCE.txt and
build.json for its exact revision. Older installers can have fewer controls;
the release page identifies each download's source and verified build.

Download ManaTable-Setup.exe from https://github.com/proflayton/Mana-Table/releases/latest
and open it. Setup installs for your Windows account, adds shortcuts, and opens
the game. Administrator access, Java and .NET installation are not required.
Open Updates from Home or Settings to check for a newer release. Startup
checks can be turned off there. The app notifies you first and downloads only
when you choose. Smaller patches are used when available; a complete download
is the fallback if a patch cannot be used. Progress distinguishes Downloading,
Preparing and Ready. You can close the panel and keep playing or building.
After leaving your game and friends' table and finishing your work, choose
Restart to update. Mana Table closes normally and reopens the same profile.
A downloaded update waits for that explicit choice, including after an ordinary
app restart. Older installed builds receive one compatible installer update to
enable smaller in-app updates.
Your decks and settings stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ManaTableNative and are retained
during updates or uninstall. Players on v0.2.7 and earlier need to download the
first updater-enabled installer manually. Everyone at a table should update.
Windows x64 only. First startup loads the card catalog.

The portable ZIP is an alternative: extract the entire folder and use its root
launcher. Keep its full layout together; do not run inside the ZIP. Managed
portable copies can offer Restart to update. Older flat ZIPs still use a manual
replacement: extract the complete newer portable ZIP into a separate folder,
or install the app. Update preparation also needs disk space for cached and
temporary files, even when the download is small.

STARTUP AND YOUR FIRST SESSION
Every launch opens a loading screen, then Home with your selected deck and its
commander(s). Loading stages follow actual startup work. Choose Play with AI or
With friends in the same place. Decks opens your saved lists, precons and imports;
the Your decks row chooses a saved list directly. + New deck starts a draft.
Choose the commander's card or Edit deck to build. Deck list is optional reading;
Back to play closes it without preparing a game or making you Ready.
There is no required review or confirmation before Play.
Opening a deck or connecting to a table shows visible progress.
Entering a game uses the same table imagery before revealing the battlefield.
Opening artwork loads before the table becomes playable, using only cards your
seat can see. The app waits until those images are ready to draw, not just
downloaded. Failed images keep readable card fallbacks. Artwork waits at most
eight seconds once the engine supplies the opening table; engine startup is a
separate stage. Other known cards in your deck warm in the background, and
ordinary table updates do not reopen this loading screen.
Deck edits show Saving/Changes saved beside the builder without covering it.
Failed actions explain the problem and offer Back; startup failures offer Retry.
An unreadable import or unavailable clipboard leaves your saved deck untouched.
F1 opens an optional controls reference. Closing it returns to the same context
without making a game decision. Games open directly at your decision, without a
first-run controls tour. Existing decks, names and play preferences are retained.
Settings opens Play controls in a side drawer, keeping the table visible.
The deck builder checks every saved edit. Problem cards are highlighted and
their reasons appear beside the selected card; deck-wide checks stay visible.
Back to play returns Home or to your connected table. Deck checks and saving
update automatically.
Your decks offers Delete beside each saved list; the builder also has Delete deck.
Deletion leaves a named Undo row and keeps the deck's cards and history available.
Open Deleted to restore an earlier deleted list, including after restarting the app.
Undo returns the list to Your decks without replacing your currently chosen deck.
Leave a friends' lobby before deleting or restoring decks. Precons and AI deck
choices are unaffected; deck management is unavailable during a game.

TUNE YOUR DECK
Open Tune beside Build, Test and Results for automatic deck checks. Browse
Checks, Engines, Swaps, Roles and History. Select an entry to keep its related
cards and evidence together; select a card to read its full face beside them.
Preferences are optional and ask direct questions about pace, format,
and cards to keep. Your deck format and land-check target start automatically.
Limited uses unused Sideboard cards as available pool cards; if no pool is known,
the review asks which cards you can use. Your playable list changes only in Build.
For a supported check, open its details and choose Test this check. The Test view
keeps a concrete observation question for your next hand sample or AI game.
Record what happened in Results, compare the tested list with your current one,
then tune in Build. Earlier tests retain their original question and deck version.
Card counts do not automatically answer turn-by-turn questions or prove a matchup.

PLAY SOLO AGAINST AI
1. On Home, select Play with AI. Choose Decks to pick a saved list, try a precon,
   import a list, or build a new deck. Imported lists open the builder.
   Select your commander and compatible partners/Backgrounds there. Changes save.
   Back to play returns to your selected deck on Home.
2. Optionally select an AI icon to choose that opponent's compatible deck.
   Otherwise Play prepares compatible opponents for you.
3. Select Play with a valid saved deck. Forge controls the other three Commander
   seats; no separate Confirm or opponent-preparation step is required.
   You make all decisions for your own cards. Auto handles routine priority.
4. After the game, select Return to setup to play again or switch to friends.
No network setup, account, API key or paid AI service is needed for solo play.

IMPORT YOUR DECK
Decks > Import deck file or Paste deck list accepts text exports.
Use quantity/name rows such as "4 Forest" or "4x Forest". Set codes, collector
numbers, foil markers and Archidekt category tags can remain in the list.
Explicit Commander tags or section headings select the commander automatically.
Import errors identify the line that needs attention and preserve your saved deck.
Use a text export for structured formats such as MTGO .dek XML or CSV files.
For an exact printing, use a row such as:
  1 Toph, the First Metalbender (TLA) 362 *F*
The set and collector number select the artwork; *F* requests foil.
Companion and Maybeboard sections currently need to be removed from the import;
the importer reports them rather than adding those cards to the main deck.
For Archidekt, choose Link Archidekt or paste a public deck URL. Review the import
before saving it. Linked imports preserve custom categories and put excluded
cards in Considering. Check for changes previews the source against your local
edits; choose a merge, keep local, use source, or save a separate source copy.
Before applying, review every affected quantity and the explanation of the new
source baseline. Keep local preserves your playable list while accepting that
baseline; a separate copy creates another linked deck. Use Previous/Next to read
all rows, including Considering. Back to review cancels without changing the deck.
Opening Archidekt and copying your playable list lets you bring card edits back
there. This export includes commander, main and sideboard; categories and
Considering stay in Mana Table. Changes are not published to Archidekt for you.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
1. Every player uses this same build. Decks opens saved lists, precons,
   Import deck file and Paste deck list. Edit deck opens your selected list.
2. Select With friends. One player selects Host a table.
   Allow the bundled Java runtime
   through Windows Firewall when Windows asks, for the network you are using.
   Choose 2, 3 or 4 Seats before hosting. Three people can
   choose three seats and play without AI; choose four to add a fourth opponent.
3. The host opens Invite friends and copies the internet invite, or an address
   for the local/VPN network. Friends select Join with an invite and paste it.
   Short a player? The host can Add AI to an open seat and choose its deck from
   the compatible bundled opponents/precons. Three people plus one AI can play
   a four-seat game. AI seats show their deck and are ready automatically.
   Remove AI opens its seat before the game so another person can join.
4. Your saved deck selection, name and commander portraits update for everyone
   at the table when you change decks or save edits. Partner commanders share
   their seat portrait. AI seats show the same public commander artwork.
   Everyone selects Ready with a valid saved deck; selecting or saving alone
   never makes you Ready. An incomplete draft can be shown but cannot be readied.
5. Play becomes available to the host when every selected seat is ready.
   Not ready withdraws your readiness. After the game everyone readies again.

Source candidate: the host can set Tempo before starting: Off (default), 120s,
60s or 30s per decision. Everyone sees the policy and readies again after a change.
The portrait clock follows whoever must decide, including off-turn responses.
It becomes a red countdown near expiry. Hover the clock rim to read its timing.
Pass + grace passes priority on timeout; required choices get 60s extra, then
lose the seat. Three consecutive unattended priority timeouts also lose the seat.
Lose seat uses the base deadline; Warn only never plays for you or removes you.
Artwork/presentation gets up to 30s before thinking time starts. Engine processing
does not consume decision time; reconnecting does not reset it. Use the same
build as your friends: older clients cannot Ready at a timed table they cannot show.

If three people are waiting in a four-seat lobby, the host can choose 3 players
under Total seats while the last seat is open. Saved decks stay selected; people ready
again after the seat count changes. A connected person's seat cannot be removed.
Only the host changes AI seats, before starting. Connected human seats cannot
be replaced, and AI cannot take over a departed player during a game. AI decks
come from the offered choices; arbitrary saved decks are not AI seat choices.
AI deck changes preserve human readiness. Returning to the lobby retains AI
decks and readiness; each human must ready again for the rematch.

BUILD AND EDIT DECKS
Choose Edit deck on Home or at your connected seat, or choose + New deck.
Search the library by name, rules text or creature type. Narrow it by card type,
maximum mana value or color identity; Leader colors follows your commander(s).
Click a card to read its large face. Use its + button to add a copy to Main.
Select an entry in Your deck to add/remove copies, click its count to enter a
quantity, or move it between Main and Side. The commander strip keeps your chosen
commander(s) visible. Choose your commander there, or use Find a partner when the
engine reports that it supports pairing. A named partner pre-fills the library
search; add that card to your list, then choose Pair with your commander.
Compatible partners already in your list are shown directly. Returning a
commander to Main keeps the card in your deck. A supported pairing is optional;
the engine still validates your final commander combination.
Other face flips double-faced cards in the preview without adding another card.

Click the set/collector button under the large card to choose another printing,
including for commanders. Browse the available artwork and foil/nonfoil finishes,
or filter by set code/collector number and cycle All finishes, Nonfoil or Foil.
then choose Use printing. All copies in that row keep their section; partners
stay in the command zone. Undo restores the previous choice. To mix printings,
choose a different printing from the library before adding its copies.
Copy list includes each printing's set, collector number and foil finish so it
can be imported again with the same choices. Exact artwork is cached separately;
when an image is unavailable, the card remains readable with rules text.

Open Tokens to choose the artwork for tokens your deck can create. Click a full
token face to save that printing immediately; Use newest returns to the automatic
newest released printing in the bundled catalog. Choices stay with this deck,
including when duplicated or used at a friends' table. Undo/Redo also restores
token artwork choices. Tokens are cosmetics and do not add cards to your list.
Ordinary exported deck-list text does not include these artwork preferences.

Changes save automatically. Beside Back to play, Saving waits for the actual
save acknowledgment, Deck saved confirms it, and Not saved offers Retry save.
Back to play keeps both chosen commanders; no additional confirmation is needed.
Undo/Redo restores edits. Deck options holds Rename, Duplicate and Delete;
Copy list exports text with commander and sideboard sections.
Card counts, a nonland mana curve and live deck checks identify needed changes.
Checks distinguish deck structure from the bundled format rules. Unknown means
the bundled data cannot establish eligibility. These checks do not certify
current tournament legality online.
Insights stay available while a draft has errors. Categories can overlap; select
a role to see its cards, or select a card to edit its categories and mark favorites.
Considering holds options outside your playable list. Swap a candidate for a main
deck card in one saved change. Versions lets you inspect a saved list and compare
its quantities with your current deck before choosing whether to restore it.
Use Test to set a question, sample opening hands, or play against AI with two
or four Commander players. Start test game prepares compatible opponents if
needed and starts the current saved version in one operation. Choose AI opponents
is optional customization. Select a sample card to read it beside the unchanged
hand; select it again or close its reading area to return to the table setup.
Hand sampling is draw-only practice; Redraw seven
does not enforce London mulligan rules. Real games use Forge's full game rules.
Results keeps observed counts and your notes with the version actually tested.
View tested deck / compare shows that test's exact saved cards; Compare to current
shows changed quantities by section without editing either list. A game ended
early is labelled separately from a completed result. Your test question is a
manual observation: use the notes to answer it from play, since aggregate counts
cannot establish what was available on a particular turn.
Tune deck returns to building; Test again sets up another experiment. Changing
the deck does not rewrite earlier results. Small AI samples are not a win rate.
For a friends' table, finish a valid 100-card deck including its commander(s),
select Back to play and then Ready. Compatible AI opponent choices persist.
Opening the builder or choosing another deck withdraws Ready; choose Ready again
when finished. Browsing, canceling the chooser, or reselecting your current saved
deck preserves readiness. Optional Deck list reading also leaves readiness alone.
The deck chooser searches saved deck names and precon names/descriptions. In a
deck review, Full deck list lets you search names, types and rules, switch between
Main, Commander and Sideboard, and select cards for a large preview. Other face
shows the back of double-faced cards. Edit this card in builder opens that exact
printing for editing. Inspecting cards or reselecting your current deck preserves
your ready status. In an AI lobby, each opponent's Choose deck opens the same
searchable picker; changing one opponent leaves the other seats alone.

TABLE CHAT AND EMOTES
Choose Player or your current name at the top to save the name your friends
will see. In a friends' table, Table chat opens the conversation in the lobby,
deck builder, or game. Enter opens chat; while its input is focused, Enter sends
and Esc closes. Typing does not play cards or pass priority; Auto waits while
the chat input is focused. Click elsewhere to return control to the table.
Use the six quick emotes to greet, thank, or acknowledge a play. Closed chat
shows an unread badge, and unmuted emotes briefly appear at the table.
Click a message to read its full text. Older/Newer and Latest browse history.
Players & mute hides one person's chat/emotes locally, or mutes the whole table.
The whole-table mute setting and display name are saved between launches.
The host retains the last 200 messages/activity entries while the table exists;
the same conversation continues through games and rematches. Chat is available
at connected human tables; AI opponents do not produce fake replies.
All friends need this social build: it uses seat synchronization protocol 3.

INTERNET CONNECTIONS
Connection lets the host request automatic router port forwarding before
creating a table. Connection shows the resulting status and addresses. A mapped status is the
router's acknowledgement, not a successful external connection test.
If your router cannot forward ports (including carrier-grade NAT), use an
existing shared private VPN with your friends, or configure forwarding yourself.
The lobby lists local interface addresses and the port. For a private VPN,
share the host's VPN IPv4 address followed by :port instead of the internet invite.
Do not share invites publicly. This is direct hosting, without a relay service.

ASSIGN DAMAGE AND COUNTERS
Assignments show the source and each recipient's current card, seat, stats,
marked damage and counters. Use +/-, click an amount to type it, or choose
Remaining to stage the available amount. Required fills an engine-supplied
assignment threshold before damage can go to the defender. Inspect a card or
View battlefield without losing your draft. Confirm assignment sends the
completed distribution; Assign later appears only when the engine allows it.
Current toughness and marked damage are separate values. A required assignment
threshold is not a prediction that the recipient will die. The engine also uses
this screen for counters and other numeric assignments; follow its amount label.

active seat. Hover highlights a card without moving or enlarging it.
Click a card to enlarge it for reading; click it again or empty table to close.

an action. The view closes when the card is no longer disclosed.
Use the opened card's current Play, Cast or ability action, or drag a playable hand
card or your commander onto the table. Each partner commander has its own Cast

Required selections, auxiliary views and alternate faces retain a read-only

Battlefield cards keep the same visual form as the table grows.

INTERRUPTED CONNECTIONS AND LEAVING
If updates stop or a connection fails, the table remains visible for inspection
and shows how old its last update is. Actions and Auto wait for a fresh state.
Reconnect checks the current table; it does not replay an uncertain last action.
The next source build retains original AI-host failures in diagnostics/ai-host.log.
A rejected bot observation also writes diagnostics/private-rejected-ai-observation.json
in your data directory. The last decoded bot batch is retained separately in
diagnostics/private-last-ai-observation.json. These failure-only files may
contain private bot hands; review before sharing. They are diagnostic evidence,
not saved playable games.
After elimination, you can watch the remaining players or leave. Leaving while
still playing concedes your seat. The host's leave confirmation explains that
closing the hosted table disconnects everyone and ends that game. Local Exit
ends the local game. Saved decks remain available after leaving or an error.

ARTWORK
The table currently uses neutral shapes and gradients, ready for artist-authored
assets. No generated arena illustration is included. Assets/ARTIST-HANDOFF.txt
lists the replaceable background, card back and optional sound cues.

LIMITS
This native client uses Forge's rules; the replacement engine is not yet
implemented. No durable match saves: keep the host and clients open during play.
All players should use the same package. Deck files persist in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\ManaTableNative. That directory also holds logs and the image cache.
Broad card interactions still need playtesting. Preserve client.log and engine.log
if you hit a blocked decision. Closing the host ends the network game.
