MANA TABLE NATIVE - BUILD, TEST AND PLAY COMMANDER

This guide follows the source revision recorded by the package. Check SOURCE.txt and
build.json for its exact revision. Older installers can have fewer controls;
the release page identifies each download's source and verified build.

Download ManaTable-Setup.exe from https://github.com/proflayton/Mana-Table/releases/latest
and open it. Setup installs for your Windows account, adds shortcuts, and opens
the game. Administrator access, Java and .NET installation are not required.
Open Updates from Home, setup or Settings to check for a newer release. Startup
checks can be turned off there. Download shows progress and can be canceled;
you can continue playing while it downloads. After leaving your game and any
friends' table, choose Install and restart. Setup waits for Mana Table to close,
updates the installed app and reopens it with the same player profile.
Your decks and settings stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\ManaTableNative and are retained
during updates or uninstall. Players on v0.2.7 and earlier need to download the
first updater-enabled installer manually. Everyone at a table should update.
Windows x64 only. First startup loads the card catalog.

The portable ZIP is an alternative: extract the entire folder and open
ManaTable.exe. Keep the engine folder beside it; do not run inside the ZIP.
Portable copies can check and download updates; open the release page to install
the app or extract the complete newer portable ZIP into a separate folder.

STARTUP AND YOUR FIRST SESSION
Every launch opens a loading screen, then the main menu: Play with AI,
Play with friends, and Your decks. Loading stages follow actual startup work.
Choose a path, then review and confirm your deck before starting a game.
Continue to setup opens the lobby. Home in setup returns to the main menu.
Opening a deck, confirming it, or connecting to a table shows visible progress.
Entering a game uses the same table imagery before revealing the battlefield.
Deck edits show Saving/Changes saved beside the builder without covering it.
Failed actions explain the problem and offer Back; startup failures offer Retry.
An unreadable import or unavailable clipboard leaves your saved deck untouched.
Short hints appear beside deck choices, Confirm, Ready and Start as needed.
At the table, a playable card explains its gesture; choosing an attacker or
blocker changes the hint to the next target. Hover Auto or Hold for its meaning.
Hints do not intercept clicks or pause Auto. They retire after a successful use
of that control, and remember it across launches. A failed action keeps its hint.
Settings > Show control hints again restores reminders without changing play.
F1 offers brief help for the current moment. Games open directly at your decision,
without a controls tour. Existing decks, names and play preferences are retained.
The deck builder checks every saved edit. Problem cards are highlighted and
their reasons appear beside the selected card; deck-wide checks stay visible.
Back to setup returns to the lobby. Deck checks and saving update automatically.

REVIEW YOUR DECK
Open Analyze in the deck workshop for an automatic review. Start with What to
check, read the benefit and tradeoff in Cards to try, then test one change.
Review preferences are optional and ask direct questions about pace, format,
and cards to keep. Your deck format and land-check target start automatically.
Limited uses unused Sideboard cards as available pool cards; if no pool is known,
the review asks which cards you can use. Your playable list changes only in Build.
For a supported check, open its details and choose Test this check. The Test view
keeps a concrete observation question for your next hand sample or AI game.
Record what happened in Results, compare the tested list with your current one,
then tune in Build. Earlier tests retain their original question and deck version.
Card counts do not automatically answer turn-by-turn questions or prove a matchup.

PLAY SOLO AGAINST AI
1. Under Play with AI, select Choose a deck (or Change deck). Pick a saved list,
   try a precon, import a list, or build a new deck. Imported lists open the builder.
   Select your commander and compatible partners/Backgrounds there. Changes save.
2. Review your commander, card counts and deck checks, then select Confirm deck.
3. Use the arrows to choose a deck for each of your three AI opponents.
   There are four precons plus green ramp and red damage starter decks.
4. Select Start Commander. Forge controls the other three seats.
   You make all decisions for your own cards. Auto can pass when you have no play.
5. After the game, select Return to setup to play again or switch to friends.
No network setup, account, API key or paid AI service is needed for solo play.

IMPORT YOUR DECK
Choose a deck > Import deck file or Paste deck list accepts text exports.
Use quantity/name rows such as "4 Forest" or "4x Forest". Set codes, collector
numbers, foil markers and Archidekt category tags can remain in the list.
Explicit Commander tags or section headings select the commander automatically.
Import errors identify the line that needs attention and preserve your saved deck.
Use a text export for structured formats such as MTGO .dek XML or CSV files.
For an exact printing, use a row such as:
  1 Toph, the First Metalbender (TLA) 362 *F*
The set and collector number select the artwork; *F* requests foil.
Companion and Maybeboard sections currently need to be removed from the import;
the importer reports them rather than adding those cards to the main deck.
For Archidekt, choose Link Archidekt or paste a public deck URL. Review the import
before saving it. Linked imports preserve custom categories and put excluded
cards in Considering. Check for changes previews the source against your local
edits; choose a merge, keep local, use source, or save a separate source copy.
Before applying, review every affected quantity and the explanation of the new
source baseline. Keep local preserves your playable list while accepting that
baseline; a separate copy creates another linked deck. Use Previous/Next to read
all rows, including Considering. Back to review cancels without changing the deck.
Opening Archidekt and copying your playable list lets you bring card edits back
there. This export includes commander, main and sideboard; categories and
Considering stay in Mana Table. Changes are not published to Archidekt for you.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
1. Every player uses this same build. Change deck opens saved lists, precons,
   Import deck file and Paste deck list. Deck builder edits your selected list.
2. Select Play with friends. One player selects Host table.
   Allow the bundled Java runtime
   through Windows Firewall when Windows asks, for the network you are using.
   Choose 2 players, 3 players or 4 players before hosting. Three people can
   choose three seats and play without AI; choose four to add a fourth opponent.
3. The host opens Invite friends and copies the internet invite, or an address
   for the local/VPN network. Friends select Join with invite and paste it.
   Short a player? The host can Add AI to an open seat and choose its deck from
   the compatible bundled opponents/precons. Three people plus one AI can play
   a four-seat game. AI seats show their deck and are ready automatically.
   Remove AI opens its seat before the game so another person can join.
4. Everyone reviews their deck and selects Confirm deck, then Ready up.
   Confirming selects the saved list for the table; it does not make you ready.
   The four-seat roster shows who has joined, chosen a deck, and readied up.
5. Start Commander becomes available to the host when every selected seat is ready.
   Not ready withdraws your readiness. After the game everyone readies again.
If three people are waiting in a four-seat lobby, the host can choose 3 players
under Total seats while the last seat is open. Saved decks stay selected; people ready
again after the seat count changes. A connected person's seat cannot be removed.
Only the host changes AI seats, before starting. Connected human seats cannot
be replaced, and AI cannot take over a departed player during a game. AI decks
come from the offered choices; arbitrary saved decks are not AI seat choices.
AI deck changes preserve human readiness. Returning to the lobby retains AI
decks and readiness; each human must ready again for the rematch.

BUILD AND EDIT DECKS
Open Deck builder for the selected deck, or choose + New deck.
Search the library by name, rules text or creature type. Narrow it by card type,
maximum mana value or color identity; Leader colors follows your commander(s).
Click a card to read its large face. Use its + button to add a copy to Main.
Select an entry in Your deck to add/remove copies, click its count to enter a
quantity, or move it between Main and Side. The Leaders tab finds commander
candidates already in your list. Set a commander, replace it, or add a compatible
partner/Background; returning a commander to Main keeps the card in your deck.
Other face flips double-faced cards in the preview without adding another card.

Click the set/collector button under the large card to choose another printing,
including for commanders. Browse the available artwork and foil/nonfoil finishes,
or filter by set code/collector number and cycle All finishes, Nonfoil or Foil.
then choose Use printing. All copies in that row keep their section; partners
stay in the command zone. Undo restores the previous choice. To mix printings,
choose a different printing from the library before adding its copies.
Copy list includes each printing's set, collector number and foil finish so it
can be imported again with the same choices. Exact artwork is cached separately;
when an image is unavailable, the card remains readable with rules text.

Changes save automatically. Undo/Redo restores edits, Duplicate makes a separate
deck, and Copy list exports text with commander and sideboard sections.
Card counts, a nonland mana curve and live deck checks identify needed changes.
Checks distinguish deck structure from the bundled format rules. Unknown means
the bundled data cannot establish eligibility. These checks do not certify
current tournament legality online.
Insights stay available while a draft has errors. Categories can overlap; select
a role to see its cards, or select a card to edit its categories and mark favorites.
Considering holds options outside your playable list. Swap a candidate for a main
deck card in one saved change. Versions lets you inspect a saved list and compare
its quantities with your current deck before choosing whether to restore it.
Use Test to set a question, sample opening hands, or play against AI with two
or four Commander players. Hand sampling is draw-only practice; Redraw seven
does not enforce London mulligan rules. Real games use Forge's full game rules.
Results keeps observed counts and your notes with the version actually tested.
View tested deck / compare shows that test's exact saved cards; Compare to current
shows changed quantities by section without editing either list. A game ended
early is labelled separately from a completed result. Your test question is a
manual observation: use the notes to answer it from play, since aggregate counts
cannot establish what was available on a particular turn.
Tune deck returns to building; Test again sets up another experiment. Changing
the deck does not rewrite earlier results. Small AI samples are not a win rate.
For a friends' table, finish a valid 100-card deck including its commander(s),
return to setup and confirm the saved list. AI opponent choices persist.
Opening the builder or choosing another deck clears confirmation and Ready;
review, confirm, and ready again. Merely browsing or canceling the deck chooser
leaves your current confirmed deck and readiness intact.
The deck chooser searches saved deck names and precon names/descriptions. In a
deck review, Full deck list lets you search names, types and rules, switch between
Main, Commander and Sideboard, and select cards for a large preview. Other face
shows the back of double-faced cards. Edit this card in builder opens that exact
printing for editing. Inspecting cards or reselecting your current deck preserves
your ready status. In an AI lobby, each opponent's Choose deck opens the same
searchable picker; changing one opponent leaves the other seats alone.

TABLE CHAT AND EMOTES
Choose Your display name at the top of the lobby to save the name your friends
will see. In a friends' table, Table chat opens the conversation in the lobby,
deck builder, or game. Enter opens chat; while its input is focused, Enter sends
and Esc closes. Typing does not play cards or pass priority; Auto waits while
the chat input is focused. Click elsewhere to return control to the table.
Use the six quick emotes to greet, thank, or acknowledge a play. Closed chat
shows an unread badge, and unmuted emotes briefly appear at the table.
Click a message to read its full text. Older/Newer and Latest browse history.
Players & mute hides one person's chat/emotes locally, or mutes the whole table.
The whole-table mute setting and display name are saved between launches.
The host retains the last 200 messages/activity entries while the table exists;
the same conversation continues through games and rematches. Chat is available
at connected human tables; AI opponents do not produce fake replies.
All friends need this social build: it uses seat synchronization protocol 3.

INTERNET CONNECTIONS
Connection options lets the host request automatic router port forwarding before
creating a table. Connection shows the resulting status and addresses. A mapped status is the
router's acknowledgement, not a successful external connection test.
If your router cannot forward ports (including carrier-grade NAT), use an
existing shared private VPN with your friends, or configure forwarding yourself.
The lobby lists local interface addresses and the port. For a private VPN,
share the host's VPN IPv4 address followed by :port instead of the internet invite.
Do not share invites publicly. This is direct hosting, without a relay service.

ASSIGN DAMAGE AND COUNTERS
Assignments show the source and each recipient's current card, seat, stats,
marked damage and counters. Use +/-, click an amount to type it, or choose
Remaining to stage the available amount. Required fills an engine-supplied
assignment threshold before damage can go to the defender. Inspect a card or
View battlefield without losing your draft. Confirm assignment sends the
completed distribution; Assign later appears only when the engine allows it.
Current toughness and marked damage are separate values. A required assignment
threshold is not a prediction that the recipient will die. The engine also uses
this screen for counters and other numeric assignments; follow its amount label.

CONTROLS
You sit at the near edge of one shared table; the other players sit to your left,
across from you, and to your right. Their cards face their seats. Spells and
abilities on the stack appear in the middle. Decks are physical piles near each
seat. Life medallions use existing commander artwork, with a lit rim on the
active seat. Hover highlights a card without moving or enlarging it.
Click a card to enlarge it for reading; click it again or empty table to close.
Use the opened card's Play card or Activate ability action, or drag a playable hand
card onto the table. Required choices, targets and mana payments use their normal
single click and stay compact. Right-click opens the complete card inspector.
Zone piles open browsers containing only the cards your player can see. Your own
graveyard, exile and command browsers can offer engine-authorized actions,
including Flashback when available. Hidden hands show anonymous backs.
Gold TAX +2, TAX +4, etc. badges show the extra mana for a command-zone recast.
Click or inspect for details. Each commander tracks its own previous casts;
dying does not add tax by itself.
Player state badges beside each life total show counters, progress such as
Speed 1/4, and statuses such as Monarch. Click a badge for its meaning and the
player's other states. MAX marks a completed progress track. Changes also appear
in the recent outcome and match history. These badges do not require activation.
Colored indicators around each player's icon show mana already in their pool,
with an exact count for each color. C means colorless. Gains pulse and losses fade;
reduced motion shows current values immediately. Click an indicator for names
and current totals. This is separate from untapped lands and other mana sources.
Mana normally empties as steps and phases end; effects can retain or convert it.
The display follows the actual pool during priority, payment and those changes.
Right-click to inspect complete rules and alternate faces during a selection.
Card pictures download when available;
text works offline. Choice galleries include a name filter and selection counts.
Ordered choices show ORDER 1, ORDER 2, etc. on both card images and text rows;
follow the decision's message for which end of the order is first.
Battlefield cards in an ordered choice spread apart so their numbers stay visible;
their usual clusters return when the decision ends.
Orders involving hand or command-zone cards use the numbered gallery.
Selecting a gallery card immediately returns its face
to the compact row; right-click to read without clearing your
selection. When every choice is a visible table card, choose those original
cards and confirm with the decision button. Other searches keep their gallery.
Engine-authorized hand-placement actions also appear beside playable hand cards;
use the offered action and any required source choice shown by the game.
Cards deal from their library piles. Opponents draw anonymous card backs into
their hands; visible spells rise into the center while you decide how to respond.
Turn callouts and life-counter impacts mark changes without blocking controls.
Reduced motion skips these animations and immediately shows the current state.
Click the current decision's instructions to read the full message.
For attacks, select your creature then the defending player (or defending card).
For blocks, select your blocker then the attacker. Repeat a pair to undo it.
You can also drag attackers to a legal defending player or card, and blockers
to a legal attacker. Arrows show assignments; blue highlights show legal targets.
Confirm your assignments using the decision panel.
Press C or select Combat to review attackers, their defenders, and blockers.
Your creatures lists eligible creatures. Select an assigned creature and use
Remove to undo its assignment; the review panel can also recall attackers.
Review blockers opens a paged gallery for each attack, with individual removal
controls while you are assigning blocks.
An invalid blocking arrangement keeps confirmation disabled and explains why.
Damage, tapping and zone changes have brief visual feedback; Reduced motion
disables the added effects. Combat arrows end at card and life-counter edges.
Choice lists are paged. For ordered choices, clear and select in the desired order.
Number, text, color and damage-allocation decisions open inside the table.
Use View battlefield to collapse a required choice, then the decision button to
return to it. History opens the game log. Click an opponent's name to open all
of their visible permanents in an upright battlefield gallery.
The table keeps every battlefield occurrence visible, including
crowded groups; enlarge or inspect an individual card to read it. Battlefield
ranks do not have pages. Long hands and choice galleries still have page controls.
Hover an Equipment or its attached creature to trace the connection. Badges and
inspection identify what it is attached to and how many attachments it has.
Current stats already include the engine's effects; do not add an Equipment's
printed bonus again. Re-equipping updates the same occurrences.
Click a player's life header to inspect public counters and commander damage
when it is not a target. Poison and other public counters stay beside the seat;
an extra-types indicator leads to the complete paged totals in that inspection.
Space confirms an enabled input decision. Escape closes a panel or cancels a drag.
F switches Auto/full control, Ctrl holds priority for this turn, F11 is fullscreen.
Auto starts enabled and passes priority when there is no playable response to
stop for. Settings include saved own-turn phase stops and reduced motion.
Empty priority windows pass quietly until your next action or a visible game event.
The bottom-right dock stays steady while the table advances. Hold remains clickable.
Battlefield cards with available actions show ACTIVATE. Hover for guidance, click
to use an ability, or right-click and select Activate ability in the inspector.
An available activation can be set to "Pass until my next turn" beside Pass.
This keeps Auto moving when that ability is your only play. The ability remains
usable; other available plays still pause the game. The indicator offers Undo,
and the preference clears when your next turn starts. Hold overrides it immediately.
Triggered abilities happen automatically; answer any choices the game presents.
For a player choice, a named recipient panel keeps the source, controller and
purpose visible. Choose an offered name or highlighted portrait. The decision
dock distinguishes your choice, your priority, another player's action and
resolution; a submitted action remains acknowledged while the engine responds.
Spells show their targets with arrows and a source/effect callout. After resolution,
the callout shows recent changes; Details keeps the explanation available to read.
New permanents and tokens show ENTERED; exiled or graveyard-bound cards leave a
brief labelled outline. Match history retains the public events.
Resolution feedback distinguishes resolved and did not resolve. Draw and shuffle
cues report actual public events, without revealing hidden card identities.
Inspecting a card, opening a panel or dragging suspends Auto. Use Full control to
inspect every priority window. Settings persist between sessions.
Settings > Text size offers 100%, 115% and 130% for larger labels and decisions.
The result screen lets you inspect the final battlefield, review history, or
return to setup. Stack inspection stays on the same spell when new items appear.

INTERRUPTED CONNECTIONS AND LEAVING
If updates stop or a connection fails, the table remains visible for inspection
and shows how old its last update is. Actions and Auto wait for a fresh state.
Reconnect checks the current table; it does not replay an uncertain last action.
After elimination, you can watch the remaining players or leave. Leaving while
still playing concedes your seat. The host's leave confirmation explains that
closing the hosted table disconnects everyone and ends that game. Local Exit
ends the local game. Saved decks remain available after leaving or an error.

ARTWORK
The table currently uses neutral shapes and gradients, ready for artist-authored
assets. No generated arena illustration is included. Assets/ARTIST-HANDOFF.txt
lists the replaceable background, card back and optional sound cues.

LIMITS
This native client uses Forge's rules; the replacement engine is not yet
implemented. No durable match saves: keep the host and clients open during play.
All players should use the same package. Deck files persist in
%LOCALAPPDATA%\ManaTableNative. That directory also holds logs and the image cache.
Broad card interactions still need playtesting. Preserve client.log and engine.log
if you hit a blocked decision. Closing the host ends the network game.

STRATEGY REVIEW
Choose Analyze beside Build, Test and Results. Your plan and favorite cards are
shared with the builder. The review starts with defaults; preferences are optional
questions you can open when useful. Review Findings, Engines, Suggested swaps,
Card functions and Decisions. Save to Considering keeps a proposed card
outside the playable list; Keep current and Dismiss record your reasons with
the deck version. Review decisions shows reviewed suggestions until you choose
Reopen this swap. Editing the playable list gives its own review while preserving
earlier history.
Automatic land targets use your highest positive commander mana value, otherwise
the first three land drops; you can choose another target. Limited review uses
your entered pool plus unused Sideboard cards, excluding Considering.
Test deck opens the existing Test setup. Review actual tests and your notes in
Results, then return to Build to tune the list. Analysis and
format checks use the bundled snapshot and show their assumptions.
