# Uncertain own-life exposure experiment

**Source-only experimental candidate; shipping v6 remains unchanged.** The
separately registered factory is `casual-commander.v6.uncertain-life-risk.v1`.
It cannot compose the library-acquisition candidate or attachment ablation,
including through the public options constructor. This document freezes the
tech-lead-approved preference on 24 November 2026; qualification and human
casual-Commander review remain pending for the exact integrated candidate.

The [original investigation](Mana-Table-AI-Punisher-Choice-Design.md) records the
real owned choice: at 12 life and four public influence counters, v6 picks an
unpriced life-loss branch over an opponent drawing one card. A controlled
continuation actually loses 28 life. Hidden library contents were never policy
input. That result proves a harmful possible consequence, not a damage forecast,
expected mana value, or an optimal threshold.

The intended behavior is that an opponent sometimes allows a card to preserve
its own life. The actual current offered index selects Forge's Draw branch or
its Mill → LifeLoss → Cleanup branch; no controls or command authority change.
The player's Palantír complaint and retained real-provider journey are the
behavior evidence. This strategy change does not claim an MTGA UI precedent.

## Frozen preference

Public facts alone cannot prove this unknown aggregate lethal: both zero loss
and lethal loss remain possible at 12 or 40 life. The candidate therefore makes
an explicit strategic preference, rather than labeling a branch unsafe.

For the narrowly certified opportunity below, let `E` be the smaller of the
public source-counter snapshot and the opponent's public library count. Allow
the opponent to draw when `ownLife <= 4 × E`; otherwise use ordinary v6.
Multiplication uses 64-bit arithmetic. Equality deliberately favors preserving
life. Four is a frozen experimental stress assumption of four life per nominal
exposed card. It is neither expected damage nor a bound, rules constant or
quantity fitted to the hidden 28-life result. No runtime weight is configurable.

At 12 life/counter4 the candidate allows Draw. At 40 life/counter4 and
12 life/counter1 it preserves ordinary v6 willingness to spend life. Counter4
at life16 allows Draw; life17 returns to v6. Counter100 with only two cards in
the opponent's library uses nominal exposure2, not 100. The formula makes no
claim that these life-loss totals will actually resolve.

## Exact supported opportunity

The current owned `OptionsPayload` must offer exactly two keyless, cardless,
mana-less choices and require exactly one. Ordering, payments, costs, hand
reduction, library acquisition and additional disclosures are excluded. Both
indices must be currently legal; absent ownership does not establish ownership.

One option is exactly one fully known, unconditional, cost-free Draw1 operation
to a singleton living opponent. The other carries the admitted
`DecisionOptionSemantics.RememberedMillLifeLoss` v1 certificate for exactly
Mill → LifeLoss → Cleanup: the same opponent mills, the explicit singleton
viewer loses life, and no other operation, payment, condition or repeated choice
participates. Actors match that opponent. The certificate binds the actual
current public source/counter and clean remembered input; operation IDs belong
only to this graph. The counter label is opaque typed data, never a card-name
or script-name exception.

The Mill/life-loss amounts and graph remain Unknown. A dependency certificate
does not create a forecast. The relationship must be an actual typed
`PlayerRelations.Opponent`; the baseline's legacy unknown-team fallback cannot
qualify. The opponent must have exactly one observed Library zone with count
at least two, and the source-counter count must be positive. Empty and last-card
libraries return to v6's existing terminal handling. Missing facts, allied or
unknown relationships and unsupported shapes also return to the existing path.
Malformed certificates are admission failures, distinct from ordinary
unsupported-world fallback.

No hidden faces, deck list or order, remembered card objects, previous hidden
result, name, label, rules prose or AILogic tag enters the candidate. It spends
the existing bounded decision work and retains host deadline/ownership/late
result handling. A routing check for absent old-wire certificates preserves
v6's exact work accounting. Present certificates add explicitly charged bounded
qualification; exhaustion cannot produce an incompletely validated Draw.

## Independent authored preference endpoint

[`uncertain-life-risk-replay.v1.json`](../../mana-native/experiments/uncertain-life-risk-replay.v1.json)
freezes eight **authored contract-input replays**, not authentic provider
captures. Both arms receive the exact same typed facts, history, seed and
allowance. Three declared protection cases use `prefer-opponent-draw`; two
known zero/one-life alternatives use `prefer-fixed-self-loss`. The remaining
three high-life/low-exposure controls are ungraded by this preference endpoint
and have separate exact-v6 proposal parity assertions. There are two declared
families; renamed, threshold and exposure variants do not create independent
mechanic families. This is development evidence, not a held-out study.

The independent `own-life-choice-preference.v1` judge checks the whole original
typed opportunity and exact current indices. It never calls the candidate
ranker, computes its four-life threshold, reads hidden outcomes or uses scores,
reason strings, policy IDs or the remaining search slice. Fixture authors
declare their preference before running either arm. A high-life frame remains
eligible if its author declares Draw preferred, demonstrating that eligibility
does not copy the candidate's threshold. That does not make the declared
preference objectively stronger play.

Known zero/one-life alternatives must retain ordinary comparative valuation.
A known five-life alternative still uses v6's existing utility. Illegal or
stale answers fail safety separately; unavailable answers cannot erase an
original eligible preference opportunity. Missing certificates are explicitly
incompatible/ungraded rather than fabricated strategic successes.

## Qualification and promotion

Focused client checks cover factory/constructor isolation, threshold equality,
exposure caps, old-wire exact proposal/work parity across seeds and tight
budgets, known bounded alternatives, current-index renewal, strict teams and
empty/last libraries. Replay-worker checks exercise the independent grader and
the actual two-arm worker on identical authored inputs. Node checks preserve
the closed factories/settings/endpoints and reject hidden-damage expectations.

Exact-input repeatability checks compare copies of identical serialized replay
bytes, history and seed; they do not construct different hidden worlds or prove
producer privacy. Two fresh provider worlds with different generated IDs are a separate
producer test with structurally equivalent entitled facts; normalizing them is
not a claim of identical raw input. Real-provider continuations must admit the
actual owned frame, evaluate once, dispatch the exact proposed current index,
record its accepted receipt and witness same-execution draw/mill/life/resolution.
Private setup stays outside native policy inputs. First attempts and cleanup
failures remain retained.

Require A/A determinism, correction of frozen protection cases, zero legality,
visibility or scope regressions and applicable exact-source CI before treating
the candidate as qualified. The existing attachment and acquisition corpora
remain independent regression controls. Later natural FFA runs require their
own preregistered plan and unchanged budgets/roster, with all planned/started
cutoffs retained and certified-opportunity coverage reported. Uncontrolled
Forge randomness does not create identical-shuffle pairs.

Repeatedly granting decisive opponent cards to avoid small losses is adverse
strategic evidence even if authored preference grades pass. Do not relabel the
frozen rubric after seeing those outcomes or fit four to them. Changes to that
assumption need a new reviewed candidate version. Human mixed casual-Commander
review remains a separate pending gate. Fixture corrections alone do not prove
overall strength or authorize shipping/default promotion or composition with v7.
