# AI experiment results, 24 November 2026

Targeted risk and acquisition corrections reproduced. In the natural pilot,
library v7 recorded five wins versus v6's six, with three unresolved time
cutoffs. These results do not justify promotion. All 108 predeclared launches
retired; 30 additional locked lifecycle-control cells remain intentionally
unlaunched.

The measured source is `148979ee29011942ae9f83dcb23b7143d44dad97`.
This report's later commit and the master/release commit are separate identities.
The study used a fresh local clean-source `0.2.0` package, engine, fixture launcher
and source-bound harness. The four packaged AI/planning/contracts/Forge assemblies
were reconciled with the harness before execution. This is not a claim that the
local files are the public installer binaries.

Shipping default v6 remains unchanged. Risk and library candidates are separate
registered factories; neither composes the other. Human casual-Commander review
and promotion remain pending. These development results do not establish general
playing-strength improvement.

## What was frozen

All eight specifications and locks were frozen at
`2026-11-25T00:02:41.2708075Z`, before any study reservation or outcome.
Replay arms received identical current provider facts and budgets. The replay
comparisons preserve original case bytes and recorded replay-format history,
plan, seed, allowance and current IDs. Owned library/attachment envelopes preserve
provider facts; their runner constructs empty diagnostic history with locked
allowances and job seeds, not captured on-policy history.
A/A verification compares full typed inputs with every numeric
lexeme retained, array order unchanged and only JSON object member order ignored.
It verifies actual commands/status, without claiming unrecorded explanation codes.

Risk uses the frozen four-life-per-public-exposure preference and eight authored
contract-input cases: five preference-graded cases in two families and three
explicitly ungraded fallback controls. It is not a hidden-damage forecast.
Library replay uses four unchanged owned captures in three families; the renamed
staged case shares its family's weight. These are known development cases, not
unseen holdout opportunities.

The natural pilot crosses four focal decks with four physical seats and two arms:
16 full-v6 games and 16 library-v7 games, each against three fixed full-v6 seats.
The pod is Rhys token/equipment, Pir/Toothy counter/ramp, Balmor spells and Traxos
artifacts. Planning seed is `261027`; policy seed is `2668620592559112008`.
Forge rules randomness is uncontrolled: these are blocked independent games,
not identical-shuffle pairs. Execution is sequential with 10-second/4096-work
decision allowances, 180-second games, 160-turn limits, 300-second workers and
16 MiB capture limits. Natural arms share the provider contract and budgets,
not identical game facts. Risk, library and attachment replays retain their
declared 3-second
allowance and 4096 work.

## Natural outcomes and reliability

The separate v6/v6 lifecycle control deliberately executed only the first two
jobs of a frozen 32-cell control schedule. Both reached verified terminal outcomes
with clean retirement; 30 remain intentionally unlaunched. This is an incomplete
lifecycle/accounting control, not a completed balanced A/A study, and equal
independent-game outcomes were not required.

| Arm | Planned / started | Verified terminal | Cutoff / unresolved | Sole / shared focal wins | Draws | Observed focal score | Planned win-share bounds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full v6 | 16 / 16 | 14 | 2 / 2 | 6 / 0 | 0 | 6 | 37.50%–50.00% |
| Library v7 | 16 / 16 | 15 | 1 / 1 | 5 / 0 | 0 | 5 | 31.25%–37.50% |

The primary endpoint is focal win share: a verified Win gives `1/k` if the focal
player is among `k` winners; a verified Draw scores zero and is reported
separately. Every arm retains denominator 16. Cutoffs, failures and unlaunched
games stay unresolved, never invented draws/defeats or removed observations.
Planned bounds are `[observed score / 16, (observed score + unresolved) / 16]`.
The candidate-minus-baseline planned difference bounds are -18.75 to 0 percentage
points.
These are missing-outcome bounds, not confidence intervals. One 32-game block
does not establish a small win-rate gain or general strength.
Library v7 did not improve measured win share in this block; these games do not
justify promotion.

All 32 first attempts retired with verified coordinator receipts and explicit
worker exit code zero. Three reached the frozen time limit and none reached the
turn limit. Critical safety, cleanup, invalid-evidence and policy failure counts
were zero. No first attempt was replaced.

Recorded gameplay rejections: 0; unknown receipts: 0; omitted trace rows: 0;
omitted replay samples: 0; pending capture items at retirement: 0. Missing metrics
would remain unknown rather than zero; all 32 supplied these fields.

Active-game time runs from start dispatch until gameplay stops, before
pre-cleanup evidence capture. Whole-worker time also includes initialization,
deck import, evidence capture and cleanup. Both depend on game workload and path.
Focal inner-policy stopwatch spans exclude
replay capture and provider/session work; they are not CPU measurements.
Grouped episodes count each evaluation once, not cumulative decision-record
elapsed values. Episode active durations remain unavailable (`null`). These
diagnostics do not establish faster policy or stronger play.

Read-only diagnostics of the three clean time cutoffs found continuing
stamps, boards and task changes rather than a repeated identical-input host
cycle. Recorded whole-game inner-policy wall spans totaled 502–555 ms (under
0.31% of the 180-second game limit), with no single span reaching 43 ms. Those
spans are not CPU or total host/provider work, and fresh revisions do not rule
out semantic strategic cycles. The candidate cutoff's final unreceipted proposal
receives no resolved-consequence credit. All cutoffs remain unresolved.

Opportunity and causal-consequence coverage counters remain `null`, not zero.
First-focal-per-task replay samples are not an exhaustive opportunity census;
absence cannot show that no acquisition opportunities occurred, and accepted
commands alone do not establish resolved effects.
